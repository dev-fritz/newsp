## [Unreleased]

### Added

- First MVP of newsp, a self-hosted, multi-user and open source (AGPL-3.0) market radar.
- Collection from Hacker News, TabNews, Reddit, YouTube channels (RSS), Lobsters, dev.to,
  Mastodon, Bluesky, GitHub, Product Hunt and any RSS or Atom feed, with a configurable interval
  per source, "collect now", per-source status and source credentials encrypted at rest.
- Provider-agnostic AI layer: local models through OpenAI-compatible servers (Ollama, llama.cpp,
  LM Studio), OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini, with API keys encrypted at rest.
- Item embeddings (768 dimensions) stored in PostgreSQL with pgvector.
- Daily market report per user, with numbered citations and awareness of the previous report,
  scheduled or on demand.
- Project ideas with why, audience, how, stack, MVP plan, monetization, competitors and evidence.
  Near-duplicates (embedding similarity plus an LLM judge) are merged into a new version with
  history and diff instead of repeating. Ideas can be deepened, edited and triaged.
- Accounts with admin and member roles, first-run setup and one-time invite and password reset
  links that work without an e-mail server.
- Optional e-mail over SMTP (STARTTLS or TLS): public signup with e-mail verification,
  "Forgot password?" and e-mailed invites. Without SMTP, development writes the e-mails to the log.
- Scheduled reports and ideas pause for accounts inactive longer than a configurable number of days.
- Web app (React Router with a BFF that keeps tokens out of the browser) in pt-BR and English,
  with light and dark themes, a fluid layout for wide screens and a feed with full-text search.
- Retention job, embedding reindex command, health endpoints, structured logs and a Docker
  Compose stack.
- Documentation site (MkDocs Material), contribution guide and security policy.
